This document records the provenance, design review history, and intellectual property (IP) due diligence process for the **Desk32** application icon.

> **Scope:** This document is a record of project provenance and due diligence. It does not constitute a legal opinion, formal trademark clearance, or a guarantee of non-infringement regarding third-party rights.

## 1. Asset & Provenance

- **Target Application:** Desk32 (`desk32`)
- **Purpose & Scope:** Local-only Hi-Fi music player for Linux (reproducing a high-end studio console monitoring environment, bit-perfect playback, 32-band GEQ, independent L/R PEQ, PipeWire / ALSA / JACK backend integration, and LV2 DSP insert slots).
- **Generation Method:** Google Gemini / AI-assisted iterative image generation, prompt engineering, material synthesis, and resolution legibility refinement (retaining C2PA Content Credentials).
- **Current Design Language:** Matte gunmetal rounded-square chassis, brushed champagne gold CNC-machined thick rectangular cuboid pillars forming a precarious balancing modern sculpture, a carved sine wave pedestal at the bottom, and condensed pure white optical glow hotspots at contact points.
- **Current Visual Concept:** Mounted on a heavy matte gunmetal plate, a thick CNC-machined pedestal carved with a sine wave groove supports three substantial rectangular cuboid pillars. The pillars intersect at acute angles, balancing in precarious equilibrium supported solely by edges and contact points. Pure white high-intensity optical glows emit from the load points, preserving distinct silhouette visibility and high-contrast recognition even at low icon resolutions (16x16 to 32x32 px).

## 2. Design Review History

### Initial Concepts (Hardware & Studio Strip Focus)

Early exploratory concepts examined literal physical equipment motifs: fader slits, isometric console strips, and analog needle VU meters. However, these elements heavily evoked Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) music production workflows. They were deliberately eliminated to preserve Desk32's distinct identity as a pure, dedicated studio monitoring playback player.

### Intermediate Revision (32 Fluted Rods & Cyan Tube Relief)

To distance the design from DAW aesthetics, an architectural plate was engineered with 32 semi-cylindrical metal rods divided by a glowing electric cyan sine wave tube, with phase-inverted specular reflections revealing "32".
While striking at 512px, **it exhibited severe degradation at small resolutions (16x16 to 32x32 px)**: the 32 fine slits collapsed into high-frequency visual noise and appeared as an unreadable dark mass in desktop taskbars and system trays.

### Current Revision (Geometric Modern Sculpture × Sine Pedestal × Pure White Glow)

To fundamentally resolve the small-scale legibility failure, high-frequency fine details were completely discarded in favor of bold, massive geometric forms:

- **Consolidation into 3 Massive Pillars**: Replaced numerous thin rods with three substantial champagne gold cuboids, securing clear silhouette retention at tiny sizes;
- **Equilibrium Modern Sculpture**: The precarious balance of pillars resting on edges captures the focused tension of acoustic calibration (precision DSP tuning);
- **Carved Sine Wave Pedestal**: Acts as the structural base, symbolizing the fundamental bedrock of audio engineering and pure uncolored tone (bit-perfect playback);
- **Sparks Eliminated for Pure White Glow**: Removed scattered firework-like micro-sparks, consolidating light strictly into condensed pure white glow hotspots to prevent noise artifacts when scaled down;
- **Gunmetal & Champagne Gold Palette**: Evokes the enduring elegance of reference-grade studio DACs and audiophile consoles.

## 4. Current Design Rationale

The finalized icon directly reflects Desk32's architectural and acoustic principles:

- **Three Massive Pillars** → 3D acoustic space, stereo (L/R) plus room interaction, and the foundational "3" of Desk32;
- **Carved Sine Wave Pedestal** → Unaltered pure tone (bit-perfect playback) and the core foundation of acoustics;
- **Precarious Sculptural Equilibrium** → The tense stillness of balancing against gravity, symbolizing surgical DSP acoustic calibration;
- **Pure White Glow Hotspots** → Signal purity, completely offline local integrity, and concentrated kinetic energy at contact points;
- **Gunmetal & Champagne Gold** → The uncompromised offline hardware block and the enduring dignity of high-end audio engineering.
